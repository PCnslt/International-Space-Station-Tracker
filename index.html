About this tracker

The International Space Station orbits about 420 km above Earth at roughly 28,000 km/h — fast enough to circle the planet about 16 times a day, once every ~90 minutes.

This map shows where the ISS is right now. Its position is fetched every 2 seconds and plotted with a glowing trail tracing its recent ground track. The Sunlight reading tells you whether the station is in daylight or passing through Earth's shadow.

Data: wheretheiss.at · Place names: OpenStreetMap Nominatim · Map: CARTO / OpenStreetMap

Tech: static HTML / CSS / JS + Leaflet, auto-deployed to GitHub Pages via GitHub Actions.
